# April desktop objects

Six original raster product-render assets generated with the built-in image tool for April's Portfolio. These are transparent 2D assets with a three-dimensional appearance, individually layered and animated in the interface; they are not WebGL meshes.

## Art direction and prompts

Common prompt: premium physically based product render, blush pink / warm ivory / champagne gold, tactile fine materials, rounded edges, warm light from upper left, near-overhead flat-lay, isolated full object, genuine transparent alpha, no captions, logos or watermark.

- **agenda.webp**: closed blush leather personal planner, stitched edges, ivory page edges, gold clasp and discreet A monogram.
- **camera.webp**: compact pink retro camera, metal trim, realistic glass lens, separate shutter and flash.
- **envelope.webp**: ivory textured paper envelope, folded triangular flap, sculpted pink wax seal with A.
- **keyboard.webp**: complete compact pink mechanical keyboard, individually beveled keycaps, ordinary QWERTY legends and cream accents.
- **candle.webp**: rounded cream ceramic candle viewed overhead, wax surface, central wick and small warm flame.
- **keys.webp**: champagne gold keyring, two keys, stitched blush leather tag and pink heart charm.

Original output dimensions retained. WebP export quality 92; alpha channel byte-checked against original PNG outputs. Combined transfer size approximately 1.5 MB. Original generation outputs were preserved during conversion. The desk composition is responsive DOM/CSS, not a single enlarged desk photograph.
